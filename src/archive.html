Usage notice

Please review the following before use.

  • Artwork rights belong to their respective owners.
  • Keep recorded images for personal use; do not share or redistribute them.
  • The developer is not responsible for restrictions or suspension of third-party accounts.
  • Review each service's terms and use this extension at your own responsibility.

By default, only metadata is recorded.

Include images in the archive?

When enabled, the record button on artwork pages and the extension popup download images. Keep artwork images for personal viewing; do not repost, share, or redistribute them.
